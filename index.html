Frequently Asked Questions

Where to buy premium oversized graphic t-shirts online?+

Joseph & James offers a curated collection of heavyweight oversized graphic tees with streetwear and retro aesthetics. COD available across India.

How do I order custom name or photo t-shirts?+

Select any tee from our Custom Category, add your text or upload instructions in the notes, and checkout. We confirm the design on WhatsApp before printing.

Do you offer minimalist text t-shirts?+

Yes, our Minimalist collection features clean typography, micro-dots, and subtle chest hits perfect for everyday aesthetic styling.

Are cropped boxy t-shirts available for women?+

Absolutely. Our Cropped Boxy collection is tailored specifically for women with a relaxed, drop-shoulder silhouette and raw hems.

Do you sell premium polo t-shirts?+

Yes, we offer premium polo tees with structured collars and breathable fabrics, ideal for smart-casual and semi-formal looks.

Where to buy anime and gaming tees in India?+

Our Anime & Gaming category features officially inspired streetwear graphics, manga panels, and pixel art tees for true fans.

What is the delivery time and tracking process?+

Orders are processed within 24 hours and delivered within 48 hours. Expected delivery time is between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Track live via the My Orders section.

What are your shipping and delivery charges?+

Orders below ₹999 incur a ₹99 fee. Orders between ₹999 and ₹1499 incur a ₹49 fee. Orders above ₹1499 get FREE delivery across India.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?+

Yes, COD is available across India. You pay at your doorstep after verifying the 4-digit delivery OTP.

Is there a return or exchange policy?+

Due to hygiene and customisation options, we operate a strict No Return / No Exchange policy. Please check the size guide before ordering.

Do you ship worldwide?+

Yes, we ship worldwide. International orders are confirmed and invoiced via WhatsApp with live currency conversion.

How do I track my Joseph & James order?+

Use the "My Orders" or "Track" button in the menu. You can see real-time stages from Order Placed to Out for Delivery.
