Frequently Asked Questions

Where to buy premium oversized graphic t-shirts online in India?+

Joseph & James offers heavyweight oversized graphic tees with streetwear & retro aesthetics, priced ₹499–₹699 with COD across India.

How do I order a custom name or photo t-shirt?+

Open any tee → enable Customise → add your text/photo note. We confirm your design on WhatsApp before printing. Details appear in your bill.

Do you have minimalist text t-shirts?+

Yes — clean typography, micro-dot and small-crest minimal tees perfect for everyday aesthetic styling. 10 styles available.

Are cropped boxy t-shirts available for women?+

Absolutely. Our Cropped Boxy collection is tailored for women — drop-shoulder silhouette, raw hem, Free Size relaxed fit.

Do you sell premium polo (collar) t-shirts?+

Yes — structured collar polo tees in breathable premium fabric for smart-casual looks. 10 colours & styles in stock.

Where to buy anime and gaming t-shirts with COD?+

Our Anime/Gaming category has manga-panel, mecha, pixel-art and gaming-logo tees. Order online, pay cash at doorstep (India).

What are your delivery charges?+

Below ₹999 → ₹99 fee. ₹999–₹1498 → ₹49 fee. ₹1499 and above → FREE delivery across India.

How long does delivery take and how do I track it?+

Orders deliver within ~48 hours. Live stages: Placed → Accepted → In Process → Ready → Dispatched → Out for Delivery → Delivered (OTP). Track via My Orders. Delivery window: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

How does OTP delivery verification work?+

Every order gets a 4-digit delivery OTP in your bill. Share it with the delivery agent at your doorstep — the order is marked Delivered only after OTP verification.

Can I cancel my order?+

Yes — free cancellation within 24 hours of placing the order from the My Orders section. After 24 hours the Cancel button is removed and the order moves to processing.

Do you ship worldwide?+

Yes. International orders are confirmed & invoiced on WhatsApp with live currency conversion (auto-detected from your location).

What is your return or exchange policy?+

Due to hygiene & made-to-order customisation we operate a strict No Return / No Exchange policy. All tees are Free Size (relaxed S–XL fit) — check the Size Guide before ordering.
