Frequently Asked Questions

Where to buy oversized graphic t-shirts online with COD?+

Joseph & James sells premium heavyweight oversized graphic tees — street, retro, cosmic & tiger prints. Free Size relaxed fit, COD across India.

Do you have minimalist text t-shirts?+

Yes — clean typography, micro dots, chest hits and blank capsule minimal tees. 10 styles, all customisable.

How to order custom name / photo t-shirts?+

Open any tee → enable "Customise" → add your name/photo note & print position. Details appear in your WhatsApp bill and we confirm before printing.

Are cropped boxy t-shirts available for women?+

Yes. 10 cropped boxy tees with drop-shoulder relaxed fit, made for women. Free Size, customisable colours.

Do you sell polo (collar) t-shirts?+

Yes — 10 premium polo t-shirts with structured collars, perfect smart-casual fits in classic colours.

Where to buy anime & gaming tees in India?+

Joseph & James has 10 anime/gaming tees — manga panels, pixel art & character graphics with COD.

What are your delivery charges?+

Order below ₹999 → ₹99 fee. ₹999 to ₹1499 → ₹49 fee. Above ₹1499 → FREE delivery. Cart shows live progress.

What is the delivery time & tracking process?+

Orders deliver within ~48 hours via live stages: Placed → Confirmed → In Process → Ready → Dispatched → Out for Delivery → Delivered (OTP). Delivery window: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available?+

Yes, COD across India. Pay at doorstep after verifying your 4-digit delivery OTP. Worldwide orders via WhatsApp.

We follow a strict No Return / No Exchange policy due to customisation. Please check the size guide before ordering.
